## Rationale (short) — proposed

### 1 — Gradle space-assignment (listing warnings)

Cloud probe with `--warning-mode all` reproduced Groovy DSL space-assignment deprecations
scheduled for removal in Gradle 10. Sites called out: `ndkVersion`, `buildConfig`, `namespace`,
`signingConfig` (debug + release), `shrinkResources`. This is the strongest static match for
Marc’s “deprecated warnings during mobile listing build.” Propose a notes-approved hygiene PR
that only rewrites assignment syntax. **Ping Justin** to confirm the same lines appear on
worktree F during `assembleRelease` / bundle, and to smoke-install after the fix.

### 2 — `js-set-map-lookups`

`expandedInstallations.includes(group.installation.id)` inside the installation-group map is a
classic O(n²) micro-smell. Home list sizes stay small, but a `Set` is a one-line hygiene win with
no UX change. Good post–`#557` identity-grid clean-up.

### 3 — `rerender-lazy-state-init`

`useState(normalizeLocale(...))` re-evaluates the initializer expression every render (result
discarded after first mount). Lazy initializer is the idiomatic fix. No i18n behavior change.

### 4 — DangerZone FlatList pair

Doctor flags inline `renderItem` and rebuilt row `onPress`. Danger Zone is a short list but it is
a high-stakes destructive surface — worth a small extracted `renderItem` / stable callbacks.
If Marc wants a broader list-component pattern, **handoff Patrick** (craft/structure) instead of
a drive-by rewrite.

### 5 — Crypto unused symbols

`generateEnrollmentKeyPair(..., securityLevel: String?)` currently ignores `securityLevel`
(Javadoc says unknown/null falls back). Constant
`SECURITY_LEVEL_CONFIRM_BEFORE_APPROVALS` is unused. Diagnosis is clear; rewriting crypto bridge
for Detekt cleanliness is the wrong trade. Propose **leave code** and either skip or
suppress-with-reason after Christophe trust glance — not a Fred drive-by.

### 6 — Play fail-open catches

`EzkeyPlayUpdateModule` intentionally catches broad `Exception` and resolves “no update” so
enrollment / auth stay unblocked. Aligns with fail-open product posture. Do not “narrow” catches
without a Play SDK exception map and device evidence.

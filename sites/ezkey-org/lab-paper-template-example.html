Abstract

This sample shows the intended reading layout for Ezkey Lab Papers: a key-findings box, a short abstract, a side table of contents (collapsible on small screens), a horizontally scrollable table, and a collapsed appendix. No experimental measurements are claimed here.

1. Introduction and motivation

Lab Papers document what happened and what was measured in an Ezkey experimental sub-project. Experience reports on the Articles lane own reflection and meaning. This example exists so editors and agents can see the HTML shape before the first real paper is written.

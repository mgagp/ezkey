Exp1 preview

Technology preview exp1 — experimental instance and invitations

Ezkey is opening a technology preview on a public instance called exp1: it is an experiment, not a production offering. The goal is straightforward — let a limited number of people try the system, collect concrete feedback, and stabilize the product ahead of the repository opening announced for September 2026 (subject to the usual preview wrap-up work).

Who this is for, and how to request access

Direct invitations come first, aimed at specific profiles (engineering, product, specialized press).

Were you not invited but still want access to exp1? Email info@ezkey.org with a few sentences: who you are and what you want to validate. Access is not open to everyone: capacity is small and each request is reviewed; a reply is not guaranteed.

What you can use on exp1

An all-in-one deployment meant for low volume and evaluation — not for load or the assurances of a production environment.

  • Ezkey mobile app (Android) — early-adopter preview.
  • Administration (tenant scope) — exp1-admin-ui.ezkey.org
  • Acme integration demo — exp1-demo-acme.ezkey.org

Android only in this phase. iOS: no release date announced.

The mobile app does not yet have the hardening expected of shipping production. App-open locking (biometrics or an equivalent strong mechanism, with control levels still to be specified) is planned soon on the roadmap; if it is missing or only partial in the preview, treat that as a transitional state, not the final model.

Access: activation code and tenant administrator role

After a request is accepted (or you are invited), an activation code is sent by email; in practice, allow up to about two business days.

Ezkey supports other enrollment paths in the ecosystem (including QR and recovery); here, the documented path is the activation code.

Administration has two levels: global (whole instance) and tenant (one organization). On exp1, you act as a tenant administrator — enough for the Acme demo, enrollments, and authentication with the mobile app.

Feedback and privacy

Feedback (flows, bugs, ideas): info@ezkey.org. Matters covered by the product policy: see the privacy policy.
